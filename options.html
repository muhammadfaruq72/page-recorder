Mobile mode

Use a mobile viewport in the current tab, filling the available height while keeping its aspect ratio. Your browser window stays at its current size.

Mobile viewport dimensions in pixels

These are the page’s viewport and saved video dimensions. The default 390 × 844 has the same aspect ratio as 1170 × 2532. The preview scales up or down to fill the available tab space without stretching. Surrounding space is excluded from the video.
