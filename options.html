Recording output

Shared quality, frame rate, and recording controls for desktop and mobile. Changes save automatically and apply to your next recording.

Shared recording output settings

Videos save as MP4. Scale multiplies the video’s width and height while keeping the page layout the same. At 3×, a 390 × 844 mobile viewport exports at 1170 × 2532. Exports can be at most 8,192 pixels per side. MP4 alignment can add one edge pixel to odd dimensions.

Higher FPS captures more motion and needs more processing power. Actual frame rate depends on the page, browser, and hardware.

More recording settings

Automatic bitrate follows the video size and FPS. Custom bitrate accepts 1–200 Mbps; a higher target generally increases file size.

Auto can use H.264 or HEVC. Selecting a specific codec requires browser and hardware support. Every option saves MP4.

A 0-second delay starts immediately. Click the toolbar icon during a countdown to cancel. Auto-stop at 0 keeps recording until you stop it; any positive duration stops and saves automatically.

A timestamp and .mp4 are added automatically. Files save in Downloads → Page Recorder.

RECORD A MOBILE LAYOUT

Mobile mode

Use a mobile viewport in the current tab, filling the available height while keeping its aspect ratio. Your browser window stays at its current size.

Mobile viewport dimensions in pixels

These dimensions control the webpage layout. Keep 390 × 844 for the mobile layout; use the shared output quality above for a higher-resolution video. The preview scales up or down to fill the available tab space without stretching. Surrounding space is excluded from the video.

PLAN YOUR RECORDING

Scroll points

Collect page positions before recording. During recording, press the next-point shortcut once for each saved position.

Turn on collection, open your webpage, scroll to a position, and click Save scroll point. Repeat in the order you want to visit them. Points are saved separately for each page URL. They use the document’s vertical scroll position in pixels; collect at the layout you plan to record.

The next-point shortcut uses your saved scrolling transition and duration, even when percentage/full-viewport shortcuts are off. Each recording starts at the first point. After the last point, additional presses do nothing. Collection supports HTTP and HTTPS websites.
